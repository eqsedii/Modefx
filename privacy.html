Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

What we collect

Your email address and display name when you sign up; your practice trades, journal entries and progress; payment records (the plan, amount, M-Pesa receipt and the last four digits of your phone number); and, for Golden members, a Deriv login ID if you choose to save one.

We never collect your M-Pesa PIN, your Deriv password or your Deriv API token.

How we use it

To run your account, unlock the plans you paid for, show your journal and statistics, prevent fraud and answer support requests.

Who processes it

We use Supabase for accounts and our database, Vercel for our server functions, and PayHero for M-Pesa payments. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

Cookies and storage

We use your browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember lesson progress and notices. We do not run advertising trackers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Kenya Data Protection Act or the GDPR) you may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Contact us through the Help centre. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Retention and security

We keep payment records as long as needed for accounting and dispute handling. Database rules restrict each member to their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
