Learn to trade without paying market tuition.

Modefx gives Kenyan beginners a safe place to practice: a simulated account, a journal that exposes habits, and lessons that fit a phone.

Why we built it

Most people meet trading with real money first and learn the rules from their losses. Modefx reverses the order. You practice with virtual money, see exactly where your decisions help or hurt, and only then decide whether real trading is for you.

What we are, and are not

Modefx is an education and practice tool. We are not a broker, we hold no client funds and we place no real trades. When you are ready for real money, you open an account with a licensed broker such as Deriv.

How we handle your money

You pay for plans with M-Pesa. A plan unlocks only after our server confirms the payment, and prices and the unlock order are enforced on the server, not in your browser.
