Risk disclosure

Last updated 25 October 2026

Trading can lose you everything you put in

Forex, CFDs, crypto, shares and indices are volatile. You can lose all of the money you deposit, and with leverage you can lose more than you expected, quickly.

Most beginners lose

Many retail traders lose money. Practice results do not predict real results, because real trading adds fear, costs, slippage and gaps that a simulation cannot fully reproduce.

Nothing here is advice

Lessons, articles, the glossary and Golden predictions are educational. They are not personal recommendations, and no prediction is guaranteed. Indicators describe what price has done, not what it will do.

Only risk what you can afford to lose

Never trade with money you need for rent, school fees, food or debts. Never borrow to trade. If trading stops being a controlled activity, stop.

Modefx is not a broker

We hold no client funds and place no trades. Real trading takes place with a licensed broker of your choice. Check that it is regulated and accepts clients in your country.
