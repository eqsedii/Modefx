Straight answers

Is Modefx a broker?

No. Modefx holds no client money and places no real trades. When you want to trade real money you do it at a licensed broker such as Deriv.

Are the prices real?

Some are. Where a real price feed is connected the chart is lined up to it and labelled. Everything else is a simulated practice price, labelled as such.

Can I make money with Modefx?

Modefx teaches and lets you practice. It cannot promise profits and nothing on the site is financial advice. Trading real markets can lose you all the money you put in.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa today, with cards and other international methods coming. A plan unlocks only after the payment is confirmed by our server.
