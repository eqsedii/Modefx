Help

Answers to the most common questions about Modefx.

Is my money at risk on Modefx?

No. Trades on Modefx use a virtual balance. The only real money you spend here is the plan price you choose to pay.

Will I make money if I subscribe?

No one can promise that, and we don't. Plans give you practice tools, analytics and lessons. Results in real markets depend on many things outside this site, and most people who trade leveraged products lose money.

How do I pay?

Choose a plan, enter your M-Pesa number, and approve the prompt on your phone with your PIN. Your plan activates once payment is confirmed. Modefx never sees your PIN.

I paid but my plan didn't activate.

Confirmation can take a minute or two. If it still hasn't activated, contact support with your M-Pesa confirmation message and we'll fix it.

Are the prices on the site live?

Prices marked demo are samples. When live or delayed market data is added, it will be labelled with its source, and AI-written commentary will be labelled separately from verified data.
