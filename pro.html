Event-day routine

  • Check the calendar each morning and mark events that touch your markets.
  • Reduce size or stand aside 15 to 30 minutes before a high-impact release.
  • Expect wide spreads and slippage at the release. Stops may not fill where you set them.
  • Let the first spike finish. Trade the direction that structure confirms, not the headline.
