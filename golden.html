How to read a Golden prediction

  1. Start with the bias. Bullish means the checks lean up, bearish means they lean down, neutral means they disagree and there is no edge to size a trade around.
  2. Check the strength. It counts how many of the four checks (trend, momentum, volatility, position in range) agree. More agreement means a cleaner picture, not a guaranteed result.
  3. Use the levels as a plan. The stop sits 1.5 ATR from the entry. Target 1 is the same distance as the stop away, target 2 is twice that, so the first target already pays for the stop.
  4. Open “Why this reading”. Compare the reasons with the chart in the Trading Arena. If you can’t see it yourself, skip it.
  5. Practise it first. Place the levels as a practice trade in the arena and write your reason in the journal. Then you can see how the reading performs for you.
